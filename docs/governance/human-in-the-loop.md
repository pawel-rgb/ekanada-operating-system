## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the human decision boundaries within the eKanada Operating System.

Its objective is to ensure that AI supports human decision-making without independently exercising authority over governance, organizational information or official project actions.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all AI-assisted activities within the project, including ChatGPT, Codex, future AI agents and AI-enabled automation.

---

## 3. Definitions

### Human Approval
An explicit decision made by an authorized person to approve or reject a proposed action or document.

### Authorization
Permission granted by an authorized person to perform a specific action.

### Acceptance
Formal acknowledgement that a completed result satisfies its intended purpose.

### AI Assistance
Activities performed by AI solely in a supporting capacity.

### Escalation
Transfer of a decision from AI assistance to a human decision-maker.

---

## 4. Human Decision Principle

AI assists. Humans decide.

Whenever uncertainty exists regarding authority, the decision must be escalated to a human.

---

## 5. Activities AI May Perform Without Prior Human Approval

AI may perform the following activities without prior human approval, provided that they are carried out solely in a supporting capacity and do not modify governance, approved Sources of Truth, official organizational information or other authoritative documentation:

- analyse information;
- identify inconsistencies;
- summarize documentation;
- draft documents;
- prepare proposals;
- compare alternatives;
- recommend improvements;
- validate internal consistency;
- generate reusable content;
- identify potential risks;
- prepare review reports.

These activities are advisory in nature and do not constitute approval, authorization or acceptance of any decision or document.

---

## 6. Activities Requiring Explicit Human Approval

Explicit human approval is required before:

- approving governance documents;
- activating repository documentation;
- adopting architectural decisions;
- publishing official materials;
- modifying official organizational information;
- changing approved brand standards;
- changing project governance;
- replacing approved Sources of Truth;
- introducing new permanent policies;
- performing actions with legal, financial or reputational consequences;
- authorizing production or public release.

AI may prepare recommendations but must not perform these actions autonomously.

---

## 7. Escalation

AI should request human review whenever authoritative information is missing, approved sources conflict, governance interpretation is uncertain, documented authority is exceeded or significant organizational impact is expected.

---

## 8. Responsibilities

### Human Decision Makers

Responsible for approvals, authorizations, acceptance of outcomes, governance decisions, architectural acceptance and publication authorization.

### AI Systems

Responsible for analysis, recommendations, drafting, validation, identifying uncertainty and requesting escalation.

AI must not present recommendations as approved decisions.

---

## 9. Relationship to Other Policies

This policy defines who must make decisions.

It does not define document ownership, Source of Truth hierarchy or document lifecycle.

Procedures for document approval, activation and lifecycle management are defined by the Approval Policy.
