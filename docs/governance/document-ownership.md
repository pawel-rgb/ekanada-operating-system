# Document Ownership

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines ownership and accountability for documentation within the eKanada Operating System.

Its objective is to ensure that every permanent document has a clearly identified owner responsible for its accuracy, maintenance and lifecycle.

This policy complements Project Instructions v1.1 and ADR-001.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent documentation stored in the repository, including governance, architecture, standards, workflows, templates, Knowledge Base, review documentation, brand documentation and ADRs.

---

## 3. Definitions

### Owner

The person or project module responsible for maintaining a document throughout its lifecycle.

Ownership includes responsibility for accuracy, consistency and updates.

Ownership does not imply exclusive editing rights.

### Maintainer

A contributor who may update a document according to project governance.

### Reviewer

A person or module responsible for validating proposed changes before approval.

---

## 4. Ownership Principles

Every permanent document must have exactly one designated Owner.

**A document may have multiple contributors but only one accountable Owner.**

The Owner is responsible for correctness, consistency, lifecycle management, review coordination, version updates and retirement decisions.

If ownership changes, the repository must be updated accordingly.

---

## 5. Document Lifecycle Responsibility

The Owner ensures that a document progresses through its lifecycle appropriately.

---

## 6. Responsibilities

### 00 – Architecture

Owns Project Instructions, governance policies, architecture documentation, ADRs and cross-project standards.

### Knowledge Base Owners

Responsible for factual information and reusable reference knowledge.

### Module Owners

Responsible for documentation within their own scope.

### Contributors

May suggest improvements and prepare drafts but do not change governance themselves.

---

## 7. Change Responsibility

Minor editorial improvements may be made without architectural impact.

Architectural changes require approval through the governance process.

Repository documentation should always reflect the currently approved state.

---

## 8. Required Metadata

Every permanent governance or architecture document should include:

- document title;
- version;
- status;
- owner.

---

## 9. Review

Owners should periodically review their documents.

Documents affected by approved architectural decisions should be updated accordingly.
