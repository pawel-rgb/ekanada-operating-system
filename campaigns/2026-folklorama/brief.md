# Folklorama 2026 Campaign Brief

## Purpose

This document defines the strategic foundation for the Folklorama 2026 marketing campaign.

It serves as the primary briefing document for everyone involved in creating campaign assets, including:

- graphic designers,
- copywriters,
- social media managers,
- Canva Studio,
- AI image generation,
- Codex workflows,
- external contractors,
- volunteers.

All campaign materials must align with this brief, the Brand Book eKanada v1.0, and applicable governance documents.

---

# Campaign Goal

Promote the eKanada Educational Program during Folklorama 2026, increase awareness of the Polish language and culture among children and families, and encourage participation in educational activities at the Polish Pavilion.

---

# Target Audience

Primary audiences:

- Children aged 5–7
- Children aged 8–10
- Children aged 11–13

Secondary audiences:

- Parents and caregivers
- Multicultural families
- English-speaking families
- Teachers and educators
- Folklorama visitors
- Community members interested in Polish culture

---

# School Offer

eKanada offers bilingual educational programs that help children and families discover the Polish language, culture and community through engaging learning experiences.

---

# Key Information

- Discover Poland through interactive educational experiences.
- Bilingual Polish-English activities for children and families.
- Open to participants of all Polish-language levels.
- Educational program available throughout Folklorama 2026.
- Visit the Polish Pavilion and learn with eKanada.

---

# Material Formats

The campaign may include:

- Posters
- Flyers
- Social media graphics
- Facebook posts
- Instagram posts
- Event banners
- Digital displays
- Landing page assets
- Newsletter graphics

Additional formats may be created if approved by the campaign lead.

---

# Languages

Primary campaign languages:

- English
- Polish

English should provide full accessibility for participants unfamiliar with Polish.

Polish should reinforce cultural authenticity and educational value without becoming a barrier to participation.

---

# Call to Action (CTA)

Visit the Polish Pavilion, discover Poland with eKanada, and join our educational activities during Folklorama 2026.

# Brand Principles

Every campaign asset should:

- represent eKanada consistently,
- support Polish language and culture,
- remain welcoming and family-friendly,
- be visually clear and accessible,
- encourage curiosity,
- reflect contemporary Poland,
- maintain consistency across print and digital media.

---

# Success Criteria

The campaign will be considered successful if it:

- increases awareness of the eKanada educational program,
- attracts families to the Polish Pavilion,
- encourages participation in educational activities,
- communicates clearly in both campaign languages,
- reflects the eKanada brand consistently,
- supports future reuse of campaign assets.
